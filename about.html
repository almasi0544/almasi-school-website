How Almasi began

Started by one man who wanted to build a school

Almasi Pre and Primary School was started in 2018 by Ramadhani Almasi, who wanted to build a school.

Today the school is run by his son. It welcomes children from Nursery to Standard 2, in small classes where teachers know every child.

Almasi Pre and Primary School is an officially registered school in Mkombozi, Kibaha.
